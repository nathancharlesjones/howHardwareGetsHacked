Why the "received_mac is only 8B" correction changes the math:

received_mac[8]  @ sp+12..19
nonce[16]        @ sp+20..35   <- sits BETWEEN received_mac and computed_mac
computed_mac[16] @ sp+36..51

received_mac and computed_mac are NOT adjacent. nonce[16] occupies the
16 bytes between them, so the real distance from the start of
received_mac to the start of computed_mac ("mac_gap" in
testing/binary_exploitation.py) is:

    36 - 12 = 24 bytes  (= sizeof(received_mac) + sizeof(nonce) = 8 + 16)

not 8. A `received_board_message_by_type()` call into received_mac with
an attacker-chosen length has to write past all 8 bytes of received_mac
AND all 16 bytes of nonce before it starts clobbering computed_mac -
i.e. the overflow has to travel 24 bytes before the two buffers it's
trying to make "equal" (memcmp(computed_mac, received_mac, 8)) actually
start to overlap.
